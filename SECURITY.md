# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Do not open a public GitHub issue for security vulnerabilities.**

Email [security@my-quilty.com](mailto:security@my-quilty.com) with:

- A description of the issue and its potential impact
- Steps to reproduce or proof-of-concept
- Any relevant logs, screenshots, or supporting material

We will acknowledge receipt within three business days.

## No protected health information in reports

Quilty is HIPAA-aligned. Do not include personally identifying
information, clinical content, or other protected health information in
proof-of-concept payloads. Describe the class of data and the access
path; do not transmit the data itself.

## Scope

In scope: `my-quilty.com` and its subdomains, Quilty-operated APIs, and
the mobile applications published under bundle identifier
`app.quilty.myquilty`.

Out of scope: social engineering, denial-of-service attacks, physical
security, and third-party services we do not control.
